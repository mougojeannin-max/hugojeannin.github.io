Applied mathematics · Bordeaux

Hugo Jeannin

PhD student at the University of Bordeaux

My research focuses on high-dimensional statistical estimation, where the number of variables is comparable to the sample size. I use random matrix theory to develop estimators and establish their consistency in this asymptotic regime.

These methods have applications in signal processing and machine learning. Applications include estimating the number of sources in array processing, as well as classifying electromyographic (EMG) signals and hyperspectral images.
